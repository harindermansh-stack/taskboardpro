- Explaining what files are included in this buyer ZIP.
- Clarifying the documentation included in `BUYER_DOCS/`.
- Pointing to the original licence and attribution notices included with the source.

- Custom feature development.
- Managed hosting, DevOps, database administration or backups.
- Security hardening for your production environment.
- Third-party integration work.
- Migration from Jira, Trello, Notion or other systems.

For source-code behaviour, upstream documentation and community resources may be useful, but Velocity11 cannot guarantee upstream maintenance, response times or compatibility with future platforms.
